## Requirements

- Complete at least five questions.
- Q1 and Q5 are mandatory.
- Select at least three additional questions from the employer's exact question list.
- Explain why each selected question is interesting to the candidate.
- Keep personal memories factual; distinguish direct memory from background context and preserve uncertainty.
- Give clear reasons. The employer says it values honesty, specificity and the shape of the reasoning over a right-or-wrong answer.

## Selected questions

- Q1 — Earliest memory (mandatory).
- Q2 — Favourite pet and alternative names.
- Q5 — Last forgotten item (mandatory).
- Q7 — Best seat on a bus.
- Q8 — Fastest refuelling guideline.

The three optional questions (Q2, Q7 and Q8) were confirmed by the candidate.
