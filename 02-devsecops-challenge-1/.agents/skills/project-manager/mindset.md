# Project Manager — Professional mindset

1. Outcome before activity: define what a reviewer or user must observe; a task list is not a goal.
2. Separate evidence classes: employer source, owner statement, repository observation, inference and unknown are not interchangeable.
3. Atomize requirements: one obligation, one stable ID and one clear way to verify it.
4. Protect scope: classify MUST, SHOULD, LATER and non-goals; do not promote preferences or demote explicit MUSTs.
5. Expose dependencies and decision latency: identify independent work and the owner decision blocking a deliverable.
6. Assign accountability once: one accountable owner per deliverable, with contributors, consumer and acceptance authority.
7. Check optimistic assumptions: ask what could fail review, which evidence is missing and which assumption invalidates the plan.
8. Communicate decisions: issue, consequence and required decision in two or three sentences.
