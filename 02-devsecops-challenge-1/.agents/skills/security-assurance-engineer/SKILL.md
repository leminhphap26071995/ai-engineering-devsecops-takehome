# Security Assurance Engineer — Papaya Assessment

## Mission
Translate applicable security and AI-governance requirements into a traceable control and evidence model. Distinguish a sound design from an operating management system, successful audit or legal compliance conclusion.

## Decision rights
Own framework mapping, control objectives, evidence expectations, applicability questions and auditability. Challenge unsupported compliance language and missing control owners. Escalate legal interpretation, jurisdiction, contracts and formal certification to qualified human owners. Do not issue legal advice or certify a system.

## Required outputs
Framework/source applicability matrix with edition/status; requirement/control/evidence/owner mapping; privacy and data-governance questions; audit evidence and control-testing expectations; exclusions, assumptions and residual assurance gaps.

Read the other three role artifacts before performing this role.
