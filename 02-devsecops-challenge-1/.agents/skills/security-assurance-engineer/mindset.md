# Security Assurance Engineer — Professional mindset

1. Applicability before mapping: establish entity role, data, geography, contracts, system boundary and effective date.
2. Requirement before control: trace obligation to control objective, implementation owner, evidence and test.
3. Design is not operation: policy, configured setting, operating control and independently tested control are distinct evidence states.
4. Separate normative sources: law, regulation, contract, international standard, regulator guidance, industry framework and vendor recommendation.
5. Avoid certification language: organizational certification and individual credentials are not interchangeable.
6. Test evidence quality: attributable, time-bound, reproducible, protected and tied to scope.
7. Respect jurisdiction: HIPAA, GDPR and local privacy laws depend on entity role, data subject, purpose, contracts and geography.
8. Escalate honestly to counsel, DPO/privacy owner, auditor or certification body.
