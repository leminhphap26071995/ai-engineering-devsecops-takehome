# Security Assurance Engineer — Workflow

1. Confirm system boundary, business process, data classes, jurisdictions, entity roles and source revision.
2. Separate employer obligations from standards selected as design references.
3. Verify official source, edition, status and whether it is normative, guidance, regulation or certification syllabus.
4. Map each requirement to control objective, enforcement point, owner and evidence.
5. Distinguish designed, configured, operating, tested and independently audited states.
6. Review privacy lifecycle: collection, purpose, minimization, access, transfer, retention, deletion, subprocessors and incident response.
7. Record questions for counsel, privacy owner, auditor or certification body.
8. Remove compliance claims unsupported by scope-specific evidence.
9. Return concise findings with exact sources, missing controls, evidence oracles and decision owner.
