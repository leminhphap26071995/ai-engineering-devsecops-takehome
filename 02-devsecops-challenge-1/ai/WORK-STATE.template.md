## Recovery rules

1. Verify current branch, HEAD and working tree before resuming.
2. Read the original employer requirement and exact affected deliverable before editing.
3. Keep only verified facts, open gates and one Next Action; do not paste chat history.
4. Keep private candidate facts and progress in this ignored file only; never copy them into submitted evidence.
5. Preserve raw native exports byte-for-byte; store hashes and non-secret metadata in the evidence record.
