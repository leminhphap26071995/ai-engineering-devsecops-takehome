# Decision record template

## DEC-XX — Decision title
- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Superseded
- **Date / source revision:**
- **Accountable role:**
- **Requirement IDs:**

### Context
Problem or constraint, with employer source, repository path or authoritative reference.

### Options
Only realistic alternatives that materially affect the decision.

### Decision
Selected approach and its boundary.

### Rationale
Decisive trade-off and why alternatives do not fit.

### Consequences
Security, operations, cost, delivery, migration and residual risk.

### Acceptance
Observable test/evidence and accountable reviewer.

### Unknowns / revisit trigger
What remains unverified and what new evidence would reopen the decision.
